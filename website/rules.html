1. Drawdown — static only

All programs use static drawdown calculated from your starting balance. If you start with $10,000 and max overall loss is 10%, you fail at $9,000 equity — even if you were up 20% before. The limit never moves up with profit.

  • Daily loss resets at 00:00 UTC (server time)
  • Floating P/L counts toward daily and overall limits in real time
  • One breach = instant fail, no warnings on eval accounts

2. Program types

One-step: 10% profit target, 8% max loss, 45-day limit. Hardest single-phase eval.

Two-step: Phase 1 (6%) + Phase 2 (5%). 11% cumulative. Most popular — still ~90% fail rate.

Three-step: Three gates at 5% / 5% / 4%. 14% cumulative. Lowest per-phase target, highest attrition across phases.

Instant: No eval. Straight to funded at 70% split, 5% max loss, 1:30 leverage. Premium fee, no free retry.

3. Profitable days (funded)

Before each payout you need minimum 5 profitable days. A profitable day = closed P/L ≥ 0.4% of starting balance. Scalping one big day doesn't count — consistency is enforced.

4. Consistency rule

No single trading day's profit may exceed 30% of your total accumulated profit during the payout cycle. Violation = payout denied, account review.

5. News trading — banned

No opening or holding trades ±10 minutes around high-impact (red folder) news for the affected currency. Includes NFP, FOMC, CPI, rate decisions. First violation = fail on eval; funded = instant termination.

6. Weekend holding — banned

All positions must be closed by Friday 20:00 UTC. No exceptions. Gaps over the weekend are your risk — we don't take it.

7. Max floating loss (funded)

Live floating drawdown cannot exceed 1.5% of balance at any moment. Breach = soft fail → account terminated.

8. Inactivity

No trades for 10 consecutive calendar days during evaluation = automatic fail. Funded accounts get 14 days before review.

9. Risk & execution

  • Max 2% account risk per single trade
  • No martingale, grid, or hedging without written approval
  • EAs and copy trading prohibited unless pre-approved
  • Reverse trading across multiple AlphaFX accounts = permanent ban
  • Lot caps enforced per account size tier

10. Payouts

  • 80% profit split (75% three-step, 70% instant)
  • 14-day payout cycle after profitable days met
  • $50 minimum withdrawal
  • Crypto and bank transfer supported
