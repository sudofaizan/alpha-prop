Questions

The short version of the rule book. Full rules live on each program page.

Getting started

Do I trade real money on AlphaFX?
No. AlphaFX is a simulated trading environment — every evaluation and funded account trades on demo capital that mirrors live market prices, so you carry zero real-money market risk. The rewards you withdraw, however, are real cash paid from your performance.
When does my account start?
Your account goes live the moment your payment clears and we provision it — usually within minutes. Trading days are counted from your first opened position, not from the provisioning timestamp.
When will I get access to the trading account?
Login details and the web trade panel are activated automatically the moment your payment is confirmed. You receive an email with the credentials, and the account also appears inside your trader dashboard.

Trading rules

What are the trading objectives and loss limits?
Each program has a profit target (except Instant), a maximum daily loss and a maximum overall loss. For example, 1-Step is a 10% target with a 3% daily and 7% overall loss limit; 2-Step is 5% then 8% with 4% daily and 10% overall. Exact numbers for every size are on the Rules and Programs pages.
Can I trade the news?
No. Opening or holding a position on the affected instrument within roughly two minutes either side of a high-impact (red-folder on ForexFactory) news release is not allowed — on both evaluation and funded accounts.
What happens to my Take Profit or Stop Loss during news?
The rule is asymmetric — a TP and an SL that fill inside the news window are treated differently. Take Profit (profit): the profit is VOIDED — it stays in your balance but is not withdrawable, UNLESS the trade was opened at least 5 hours before the event (a genuine swing), in which case it counts as normal payable profit. Stop Loss (loss): the loss COUNTS in full toward your daily and total drawdown — no void, no relief. A manual open or close inside the window is also a rule violation. In short: news profits do not count, news losses do.
